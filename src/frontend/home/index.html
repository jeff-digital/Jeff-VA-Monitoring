The workspace

Keep your work organized.

Bring the details of your job search and client work together, with a clear view of what needs attention.

01

Job applications

Track opportunities, application statuses, interviews, and follow-ups. Keep your next steps visible as your search progresses.

02

Client management

Keep client details, email history, contracts, and documents together. Check local time zones when planning your work.

03

Tasks and deadlines

Organize daily and client tasks, record work hours, and keep track of deadlines and completed work.

04

Connected email

Connect Gmail, send messages, and match conversations to clients and applications without losing the context.

05

Invoices and finances

Manage invoices and recorded payments. Track income, expenses, balances, and subscription dates alongside your work.

06

Reports and documents

Review progress through dashboards and weekly reports. Organize files, reuse templates, and export workspace backups.

Automation

A few things off your list.

Connect emails, tasks, and application records with automatic checks and the schedules you choose.

CapabilityHow it connectsWhat it handles

Email to task

  1. Client request
  2. AI review
  3. Task created

Recognizes active-client work requests and captures clear deadlines. Uncertain requests can be flagged for review.

Scheduled follow-ups

  1. Set a date
  2. Sending window
  3. Email sent

Sends enabled Gmail follow-ups during the client's weekday business hours, with duplicate-send protection.

Application updates

  1. Matched reply
  2. Outcome identified
  3. Status updated

Uses recognized email outcomes to update eligible application records.

Scheduled backups

  1. Your schedule
  2. Backup due
  3. Excel download

Downloads a workspace export when your enabled backup schedule is due.

Email features require connected Gmail. Automatic checks run while the app is open. Backup downloads depend on browser permissions.
